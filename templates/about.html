About This Project

A deep learning classifier for brain tumor detection from MRI scans, built with TensorFlow and deployed with Flask.

Classes
Glioma Tumor
Tumors arising from glial cells in the brain or spine. Accounts for ~33% of all brain tumors. Includes glioblastoma, astrocytoma, and oligodendroglioma.
Meningioma Tumor
Arises from the meninges surrounding the brain and spinal cord. Most are benign and slow-growing. Most common primary brain tumor.
Normal (No Tumor)
No signs of tumor present. Brain tissue appears healthy with no abnormal masses detected in the MRI scan.
Pituitary Tumor
Forms in the pituitary gland at the base of the brain. Most are benign adenomas. Can affect hormone production.
Training Results
Training Pipeline

The model was trained in Google Colab using a two-phase approach. Phase 1 froze the MobileNetV2 base and trained only the custom classification head. Phase 2 unfroze the top layers for fine-tuning with a lower learning rate. Data was manually split 70/15/15 into train/val/test sets with light augmentation (horizontal flip, 5° rotation, 5% zoom).

The dataset was sourced from Kaggle (thomasdubail/brain-tumors-256x256) and contains 256×256 grayscale MRI images resized to 224×224 for MobileNetV2 compatibility.
